Help

Some pages flash, pulse, or move quickly. Close the page if that is uncomfortable. Smart lights are optional.

Start

  1. Open this project's folder.
  2. Right-click an empty spot and choose Open in Terminal.
  3. Type python controller/serve.py and press Enter. If that fails, try py controller/serve.py.
  4. Leave the terminal open. In your browser, visit http://127.0.0.1:8800/.

The House

  • Press any key to begin.
  • Up and Down look through choices.
  • Enter travels, opens, or confirms.
  • Escape goes back.
  • H stirs the colour and movement.
  • There is no score and no wrong path.

Party

  • Hold Up for more energy; hold Down for less.
  • Press P to change steering mode.
  • Press lowercase h to preview the next visual.
  • Hold Shift and press H to keep the preview.
  • Press D to show or hide status information.

Games

Up/Down or W/S moves. Enter or Space selects. Backspace or Left Arrow goes back. Escape leaves the game.

Big screen

Press F11 to enter or leave full screen. For two screens, open house.html in one window and display.html in another. Put the display window on the television or projector.

Phone or another device

The main experience runs on the computer. A phone or second device is optional and must use the same Wi-Fi.

  1. On Windows, open Command Prompt.
  2. Type ipconfig and press Enter.
  3. Find IPv4 Address.
  4. On the computer, open http://YOUR-IP:8800/qr/ with that number in place of YOUR-IP.
  5. Right-click the launcher in Chrome or Edge and choose Create QR code for this page.
  6. Scan the QR code with the phone's Camera app.

Grey cards marked Not in public version are shown for completeness but cannot be opened.

Stop

Close the browser windows. Return to the terminal, hold Ctrl, and tap C.
