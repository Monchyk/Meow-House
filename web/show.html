MeowParty · WHITE CAT CALLING · the show so far

One organism.
You play its states.

A cat scoots a spiral tunnel of lit tiles; the room breathes to a real toy train's loop; someone walks in and the piece leans toward them. It's not a slideshow of scenes — it's one living state you dial, from calm to chaos and back, and the scenes fall out of where you take it.

The states you play

Calm · regulated

"calm, cool, starlike"

σ high, business low — the still ordered mandala.

Dysregulated · chaos

"crazy or calm"

σ low, business high — off-kilter, churning.

The swing

"symmetry to chaos… slow to a crawl… loop, reloop"

the move between, swept slow.

The swell · breath

"a long swell that recedes and returns — it asks, it never shouts"

the train clock's body.

Standstill → asking

left alone it stills, flashes inward for attention, then dies out.

The realisation

"reads as a realisation, not a glitch"

the slow earned resolve — "the whole piece."

The mismatch

surface vs hidden — the mask that slips. What's shown ≠ what's felt.

The dive · void

"the tunnel keeps going into black"

going through, never arriving.
