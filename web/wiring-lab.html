Same seed, same run — placement, branching and wander are all seeded, and the wander is a noise field indexed by (cell, time), so it replays frame for frame. Every cell spawns its own hue and keeps it. Colour does not fuse — it bleeds one hop at a time along the filaments, hardest where two differently-coloured regions meet and fainter with every hop away from that seam, while each cell pulls elastically back toward the hue it was born with. Mixing is asymmetric, allele-style: a dominant hue moves its neighbours more than they move it. So the lattice settles into a gradient, not one colour — cells at a junction go most of the way to their neighbour, the far tips stay as they started. Chroma is never part of the mix (a hue only becomes pixels at the most saturation sRGB holds), so nothing goes grey. Migration speed and branch angles are sampled from research/organisms/cell-motion.json, measured off the real culture footage by tools/extract_cell_motion.py.
